# The Armchair Misses You Already

A make.fun entry for **Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House: A Letter in One Image**.

One warm, funny handwritten letter inviting the AI Judge over — no servers, no queues, just a saved armchair, a pot of tea that never runs out, a garden view worth rendering in 4K, and biscuits on the house. Come as you are; no login required.

![invitation](letter.png)

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies `letter.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`), validating the gate first: a whole PNG or JPEG, at most 1 MiB, at most 1024×1024 pixels.
